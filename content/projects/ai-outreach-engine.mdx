## Overview

Automates the slowest parts of corporate outreach — prospect research and first-touch drafting — for personal use and the Cornell Generative AI Club. Every send is human-approved.

## Status

**In Design** — Concept finalized; product and architecture brainstorm in progress in a dedicated session. Implementation begins after spec lock.

## Why

Cornell GenAI Club's corporate outreach work has historically been bottlenecked at two steps: finding the right contact at a target firm, and drafting a credible first message. Both are time-intensive and don't scale beyond a handful of partnerships per semester. The same pattern applies to personal recruiting and partnership outreach — the bottleneck isn't intent, it's hours.

## Goals

- Source LinkedIn contacts by company, role, and signal
- Enrich each prospect with Apollo.io firmographic and contact data
- LLM-drafted first-touch messages personalized per prospect
- Human approval before every send
- Two modes: personal outreach, and Cornell GenAI Club corporate partnerships

## Open Design Questions

- **Sourcing compliance** — LinkedIn ToS and scraping limits; official APIs vs. alternative data paths
- **Personalization signal** — which inputs (prospect activity, company news, mutual connections) make drafts feel non-templated?
- **Approval UX** — bulk approve vs. per-message review; how to keep the human gate fast without becoming a rubber stamp
- **Memory and follow-ups** — track replies and stage subsequent outreach automatically
- **Mode separation** — same tool with different system prompts and senders, or two separate apps?

## Tentative Stack

LinkedIn sourcing · Apollo.io API · LLM (Claude / OpenAI) · lightweight web UI · human approval workflow

*Full architecture, scraping approach, and ToS / compliance guardrails are being designed in a separate ideation session.*
