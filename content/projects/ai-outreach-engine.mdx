## Overview

Automates the slowest parts of outreach, finding the right contact and drafting a credible first message, for my own recruiting and for the Cornell Generative AI Club's corporate partnerships. Every message is human-approved, and nothing is sent automatically.

## Status

**In Progress.** The outreach layer (v1) is complete: campaigns, prospect import, drafting, review, and Gmail and Google Sheets integration, with an end-to-end test. A job board that tracks postings from my internship scraper is merged, and linking job postings to sourced contacts is in progress.

## Why

Outreach bottlenecks at two steps: finding the right person at a target firm, and writing a first message that does not read as a template. Both take hours and do not scale past a handful of conversations a week. The bottleneck is not intent, it is time.

## How It Works

- **Import.** Prospects come in as CSV from free-tier sourcing tools. No scraping and no LinkedIn automation, so there is zero account risk.
- **Warmth scoring.** Each prospect is scored on shared affiliations (school, program, clubs) so the warmest contacts surface first.
- **Dual-mode drafting.** An LLM drafts in one of two tuned modes: personal career outreach, or GenAI Club partnership outreach with a per-campaign goal (sponsorship, speaker, partnership, recruiting).
- **Human gate.** A keyboard-driven review queue with inline editing and approve or reject. Approved emails become Gmail drafts; LinkedIn messages are copied by hand.
- **Tracking.** Sent messages auto-populate a Google Sheets tracker for each mode.

## Job Board

A `/jobs` board ingests postings from my internship scraper, with triage zones, table and kanban views, filters, and deadline tracking. The next step links an accepted posting directly to contacts at that company, so applying and reaching out happen in one place.

## Tech Stack

Next.js, TypeScript, Prisma, Postgres (Neon), LLM APIs, Gmail API, Google Sheets API, Vitest.
