## Overview

I spent Summer 2026 as a Business Process Improvement and AI Intern at American Electric Power, one of the largest utilities in the US. The work split two ways: strategy research for an enterprise robotics initiative, and building AI tools for teams that had never used them.

Everything shipped inside a real corporate environment: locked-down machines, an internal LLM gateway with early access, and users who needed results, not setup instructions. Most of the interesting decisions came from those constraints.

## How the build work fit together

Early in the summer I built a small Python client that made the company's internal LLM gateway usable in two lines of code with no setup. Every build project after that ran on top of it, including tools other teams adopted.

## Constraints that shaped the work

## Also shipped

- **AI Office Hours.** A self-initiated 5-week program teaching teammates Python and LLM fundamentals, with a reference guide for each session.
- **Invoice inbox monitor.** Daily collection and scheduled reports on a shared invoice inbox, giving the accounting team visibility it did not have before.
- **Rate-sheet Excel stage.** The final stage of a multi-person pipeline that turns contractor rate proposals into standard spreadsheets. I defined the data contract with teammates, integrated end to end, and handed it off.
